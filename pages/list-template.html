1. Cover Your Tracks is a privacy tool by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) that analyzes your browser’s unique fingerprint to reveal how easily websites can track you online.

    It tests for tracking technologies like cookies, fingerprinting, and other data collection methods, providing a detailed report on your browser’s privacy defenses. The site helps users understand their digital footprint and offers insights on improving anonymity while browsing the web.

  2. Atlas of Surveillance is a project by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF) that maps the use of surveillance technologies by law enforcement agencies across the United States.

    It provides a searchable database of tools like facial recognition, license plate readers, and predictive policing software, helping the public understand the growing landscape of government surveillance. The site encourages transparency and accountability by compiling data from public records, news reports, and crowdsourced research.

  3. Surveillance Self-Defense (SSD), created by the Electronic Frontier Foundation (EFF), provides practical guides and resources to help individuals protect their privacy and security online.

    It offers step-by-step tutorials on encryption, secure communication, device protection, and defending against government and corporate surveillance

  9. Apiary for Practical Support serves as a national hub, offering resources and technical assistance to organizations that provide logistical aid to individuals seeking abortions. By collaborating with these groups, Apiary aims to enhance the effectiveness and sustainability of practical support services across the country.

Repro Legal Helpline offers free, confidential legal services related to abortion, pregnancy loss, and birth, assisting individuals with judicial bypasses, emergency abortion denials, and potential criminalization of pregnancy outcomes. Operated by If/When/How, the helpline provides resources tailored to various needs, including information for young people seeking abortions.
