### Narrative Voices
- **Primary Narrator:** The text is written in the first person by a female narrator who expresses her own sexual preferences, beliefs, and identity. She is unapologetic and explicit in her expression.
- **Implicit Male Voice:** While not directly speaking, the perspective and desires of "men" are frequently referenced and framed as authoritative and dominant, contributing to a dual-voice dynamic where the narrator often aligns herself with male perspectives on sexual dominance and subjugation.

### Narrative Arcs and Threads
- **Sexual Submission and Objectification Arc:** The narrator describes a trajectory of willingly submitting to male domination, celebrating being used, mistreated, and having her pleasure subordinated or denied.
- **Distinction Arc (Self vs. "Normal" Women):** A thread of differentiation runs throughout, whereby the narrator repeatedly contrasts herself as "not normal," particularly emphasizing her obesity and corresponding sexual treatment compared to other women.
- **Biological/Animalistic Frame Arc:** The narrator frequently reverts to biological determinism and animalistic imagery, presenting human sexual behavior as natural and inevitable, rooted in reproduction and primal drives.

### Recurring Imagery and Motifs
- **Physical Invasion and Filling:** Repeated imagery of bodies being filled — especially holes being "filled," "creampied," "fatter," and "full and creamy" — conjures visceral sensations and symbolizes submission and possession.
- **Animalistic/Primal Imagery:** References to "wild animals in heat," "fertilized and then abandoned," and "nature intended" evoke primal sexual roles and instincts.
- **Degradation and Humiliation:** The narrator frequently embraces themes of mistreatment, denial of pleasure, and humiliation, casting these as desirable and validating them as natural.
- **Male Dominance and Ownership:** Men are envisioned as possessing ownership of women’s bodies, exerting control and superiority, framed in imagery of control, refusal, and enforcement of women’s submission.
- **Denial of Female Pleasure:** Motifs around clitoral removal and denial of female orgasm highlight a conceptual motif of eradicating women's sexual autonomy and pleasure for male-centric sexual function.

### Conceptual Frames
- **Biological Determinism:** The text posits an essentialist view of sex and gender, where sexual roles and behaviors are predetermined by nature, biology, and evolutionary imperatives.
- **Patriarchal Sexual Hierarchy:** The narrator adopts a worldview wherein men are sexually superior, controlling agents, and women (especially fat women) exist to serve male desire and reproductive purposes.
- **Sexual Ownership and Subjugation:** A frame is presented wherein women’s bodies are owned property in sexual contexts, with male authority justifiably suppressing female desire and autonomy.
- **Nature as Justification:** Natural law and "Mother Nature" are repeatedly invoked to justify sexual roles, subjugation, pregnancy outcomes, and social consequences such as single motherhood.
- **Rejection of Female Sexual Pleasure:** The denial of female orgasm and removal of the clitoris as a "gift" to men is framed as a rational, loving act consistent with this worldview, reinforcing male priority in sexual relations.

### Cognitive Styles
- **Absolutist and Monolithic Reasoning:** The narrator holds rigid, black-and-white beliefs about sexual roles, women’s worth, and men’s rights without room for nuance or alternatives.
- **Extreme Self-Identification:** The narrator strongly identifies with marginalized identity traits (e.g., obesity) and integrates these into her sexual self-concept via an extreme framework of deserved mistreatment and denial.
- **Rationalization of Submission:** The text provides detailed justifications for submission and degradation, reflecting a cognitive style focused on logical coherence within a self-imposed, socially deviant framework.
- **Emotional Detachment and Resignation:** While explicit sexual pleasure in service of men is highlighted, there is an undercurrent of emotional resignation, acceptance of hardship, and detachment from traditional notions of female empowerment or equality.
- **Prescriptive Voice:** The narrator expresses normative judgments about both herself and other women, prescribing behaviors and beliefs that conform to the ideological framework she advocates.
